Chicken Pot Pie

Home

Description

This chicken pot pie is the ultimate comfort food: a flaky, golden crust wrapped around a creamy filling of tender chicken, carrots, peas, and potatoes. The filling is thickened with a simple homemade sauce flavored with thyme and a touch of white wine or broth.

Using a store-bought crust keeps it easy enough for a weeknight, and rotisserie chicken saves even more time. Let the pie rest for 10 minutes after baking so the filling sets. Serves 6 to 8.

Ingredients

  • 2 refrigerated pie crusts (9-inch), or 1 double-crust recipe
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 2 medium carrots, peeled and diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 medium Yukon Gold potato, peeled and diced small
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1¾ cups chicken broth
  • ¾ cup whole milk
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 3 cups cooked chicken, shredded or cubed
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1 large egg, beaten with 1 tablespoon water (for egg wash)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Fit one pie crust into a 9-inch deep-dish pie plate and set aside.
  2. Melt the butter in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, celery, and potato and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, until the vegetables begin to soften.
  3. Stir in the garlic and cook for 1 minute, until fragrant.
  4. Sprinkle the flour over the vegetables and stir constantly for 1 to 2 minutes to cook out the raw flour taste.
  5. Slowly pour in the chicken broth and milk, stirring continuously. Add the thyme, salt, and pepper.
  6. Simmer for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring often, until the sauce is thick and the potatoes are tender.
  7. Stir in the chicken and peas. Taste and adjust the seasoning, then remove from the heat.
  8. Pour the filling into the prepared pie crust and spread it evenly.
  9. Place the second crust over the top, trim the excess, and crimp the edges to seal. Cut 4 small slits in the top to let steam escape.
  10. Brush the top crust with the egg wash.
  11. Place the pie on a baking sheet to catch any drips and bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until the crust is deep golden brown and the filling is bubbling.
  12. Let the pie rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
